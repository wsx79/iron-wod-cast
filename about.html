IRON WOD

A cross-training workout timer, workout log and training-analysis app for Android.

IRON WOD helps athletes run structured workouts (AMRAP, EMOM, Tabata, For Time, custom intervals and more), log results, track progress over time, and follow along on a TV or Chromecast display during a session.

What it does

  • Structured workout timers with voice and sound cues (AMRAP, EMOM, Tabata, For Time, custom intervals)
  • Workout logging and history, with performance and progress analytics
  • A built-in workout/exercise library, with support for custom WODs
  • Optional heart-rate tracking via compatible Bluetooth devices
  • Casting the live timer to a TV via Google Cast or a wired HDMI/USB-C connection
  • Optional backup of a user's own workout data to their own Google Drive account

Your data

IRON WOD stores your training data on your device. It does not require an account, and does not run its own cloud backend. If you turn on the optional Google Drive backup, your data is saved only to a private area of your own Google Drive that the developer cannot access. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

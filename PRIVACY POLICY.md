# Privacy Policy — IRON WOD

**Last updated:** 13 August 2026

IRON WOD (“the App”) is a local workout timer and training log for Android.

## Data we store on your device
The App stores the following **only on your device**:
- Workout sessions, results and history
- Custom WODs and exercise catalogue preferences
- Max lifts and related training notes
- App settings (language, units, timer preferences, voice/sound options)

The App does **not** require a user account and does **not** upload your training history to a server operated by the developer.

## Permissions
- **Vibrate** — timer feedback during workouts
- **Nearby Wi‑Fi devices** — optional Google Cast / Chromecast projection
- **Internet** — used when you enable Cast, and when the App or the Cast receiver needs to load the online resources described below

## Chromecast / Google Cast
If you use the Cast feature:
- The App communicates with **Google Cast / Google Play services** on your network, according to Google’s own policies.
- A **custom Cast web receiver** shows the timer on the TV. That receiver is hosted publicly at:

  **https://wsx79.github.io/iron-wod-cast/**

  (GitHub Pages). Related static assets used by the receiver (for example HTML, JavaScript, CSS and optional voice audio files) are loaded from that same GitHub-hosted location.
- When Cast is active, the TV/receiver may download those public assets over the Internet. This is limited to resources needed to display and announce the timer. It is **not** used to upload your workout history to GitHub or to the developer.
- You can avoid this network use by not using Cast.

## Other third parties
- Optional links you choose to open (for example support or music links) leave the App and are governed by those services’ policies (e.g. PayPal, Spotify).

## Backup
If Android Auto Backup or device transfer is enabled on your phone, App data may be included in your system backup according to your device/Google settings.

## Children’s privacy
The App is intended for adults and athletes. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices
- Export or clear data using in-app tools where available.
- Uninstalling the App removes local App data (except copies kept in system backups you control).
- Disable Cast if you do not want the receiver to load assets from the network/GitHub.

## Contact
For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on the Google Play store listing.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change when we do.
